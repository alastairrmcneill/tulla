5iPad — dark, landscape, three-pane coach view

The tablet isn't a stretched phone: it's the coach's tool, so the layout is built around seeing the squad and one athlete at the same time. Left is a glass icon rail, middle is the persistent squad list (flagged first, then everyone), right is the detail pane. Selecting a name never loses your place in the list — the whole point of the split. Squad signals gets the space it deserves here: all five questions as full charts in a 2-up grid rather than sparkline rows, each with its distribution and the "worth trying" prompt inline. The athlete check-in exists too (Today in the rail) but stays a centred single column at phone measure — a 1,100px-wide row of five tap targets would be worse, not better. Glass stays restrained: rail, sheet and FAB only. REDUCED in the top bar swaps every glass surface for its solid fallback.
