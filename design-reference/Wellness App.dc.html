3Full prototype on 2a — onboarding → paywall → athlete → coach

Rebuilt on Night training: near-black ground, electric lime accent, all-caps micro-labels, oversized numerals, glass only on the tab bar, sheets and the FAB. Flag rows use 1b (severity icon + reason chips). Turns 1–2 are preserved in Wellness App v1 (turns 1-2).

Your scale legends: each row header carries an ⓘ. The row shows the short label, and once answered, that number's exact wording in lime on the right. Tapping ⓘ opens a glass sheet with the long question and all five response strings — and each one is tappable, so the sheet doubles as a slower, fully-worded input path for anyone who wants it. Copy is verbatim from your Question model (two typos fixed: "Restless", and the double space in Mood 3).

Walk it with the pill bar above the phone — or start at Welcome and go all the way through. The coach path gates on the trial exactly as specced: paywall → trial → team creation → Team Home, with the lockout screen reachable from the trial banner.
